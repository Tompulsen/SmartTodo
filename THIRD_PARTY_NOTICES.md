## Publication assets

- Audio-library covers, the focus/rest illustrations, and the favicon are
  generated by `scripts/create-public-art.ps1` using basic geometric drawing.
- Built-in WAV ambience and the music-box melody are synthesized by
  `scripts/create-focus-sounds.py`.
- Completion sounds are generated by `scripts/create-alert-sounds.py`.
- These generated assets are included under this repository's MIT License.
- User reference screenshots, cropped reference illustrations, old third-party
  MP3 effects, and personal audio files are not included in this public edition.

The source and runtime behavior are preserved; the public edition's generated
artwork differs from the private local customization.
